Howdy @lsalemme - Kay here from the Marketplace team To address your questions: No PvV on Forge: Forge apps on the Atlassian Marketplace can only be listed as Free or Paid via Atlassian (PvA) . There is no Paid-via-Vendor option for Forge. To monetize, you will need to list as Paid via Atlassian (PvA) .
